## Overview
Lab 5 for CS1566 was to generate a triangle and through a vertex shader have that triangle move around the screen by pressing left click on the mouse and dragging, and to also temporarily resize it by holding down right click.

## Tech Stack
- Programming: C, OpenGL
- Text Editor: VSCode

## Takeaways
I remember this lab being a lot of fun! It was cool to finally be able to interact with a graphics application that I built. It took a while for me to understand what a vertex shader is and what it's even doing to the triangle to the screen. I kept asking myself if the matrix storing it's transformation data was the same as the raw vertex data, I kept confusing the two a lot. Eventually, I understood that the vertices to create the triangle should stay as they were generated and have the transformation matrix handle all the translation, rotation, and scaling.
